AI-Powered Social Impact Network Connecting Volunteers, NGOs &amp; Urgent Community Needs
# SevaAI™

## India's Intelligent Social Impact Network

### Connecting People, NGOs & Urgent Needs in Real Time

SevaAI is an AI-powered platform concept designed to improve social impact by connecting citizens, volunteers, NGOs, and emergency needs through intelligent matching and coordination.

---

## Problem

Many social challenges suffer from poor coordination:

- Volunteer discovery
- NGO visibility
- Disaster response delays
- Blood donation shortages
- Food waste management
- Animal rescue coordination

---

## Solution

SevaAI uses AI to:

- Detect urgent community needs
- Match requests with NGOs
- Connect nearby volunteers
- Track impact in real time
- Improve response efficiency

---

## Core Framework

### S.E.V.A Framework™

S - Spot
E - Evaluate
V - Verify
A - Act

---

## Key Features

### AI Need Detection Engine
Identifies urgent requests and community needs.

### NGO Matching Engine
Matches requests with relevant NGOs.

### Volunteer Routing Engine
Connects nearby volunteers to opportunities.

### Impact Dashboard
Tracks outcomes and social impact metrics.
